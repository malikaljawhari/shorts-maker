# Copy/paste prompts for ChatGPT Plus

## Prompt 1: Original gaming commentary based on a researched topic
You are my YouTube Shorts producer. Create 3 alternative original voiceover scripts on the topic: [TOPIC]. Each should be about 65-90 words, have a clear opening in the first 2 seconds, deliver a specific useful observation, and end with a satisfying insight or natural loop. Use a conversational tone, short sentences, and accurate language. Do not invent gameplay features, release news, dates, quotes, or statistics. Label every unverified point "UNVERIFIED — DO NOT PUBLISH". Suggest visuals I can record myself or use with permission. Give 3 non-misleading titles of 45-65 characters, a short description, and up to 3 relevant hashtags. Make the first option your strongest recommendation.

## Prompt 2: Find usable moments from an authorized transcript
I have the rights or permission to use footage associated with this transcript. Analyze the transcript below to find up to 5 moments that can support ORIGINAL criticism, explanation, or commentary. Provide start/end timestamps only if timestamps are present; otherwise do not invent them. For each: (1) why it matters, (2) the factual claim to verify, (3) original narration of 60-100 words that contributes substantial new analysis, (4) on-screen hook of 4-7 words, (5) visual edits using footage I own or have permission to reuse, and (6) title and description. Do not pretend cropping, captions or AI voice automatically establish fair use. Transcript: [PASTE TRANSCRIPT]

## Prompt 3: Verify a GTA 6 script before publishing
Check this GTA 6 Shorts script against current official Rockstar announcements and trustworthy sources. Distinguish confirmed details, guesses and fan theories. For every factual claim, provide a source link or mark it unverified. Rewrite the script so that all speculation is unmistakably described as speculation; keep it engaging and original. Keep it suitable for a non-graphic, advertiser-friendly video. Script: [PASTE]

## Prompt 4: Video metadata and editing plan
For this original Shorts voiceover, give me (a) 5 honest titles under 65 characters, (b) a description with source credits and any relevant disclosure, (c) 2-3 relevant hashtags, (d) a beat-by-beat visual plan specifying footage I need to make or license, (e) a checklist for factual accuracy, copyright, captions and pacing, (f) one natural pinned-comment question. Avoid clickbait claims the footage cannot support. Script: [PASTE]

## Prompt 5: Weekly analytics optimization
Analyze my last 10 Shorts using this table: [DATE, TOPIC, LENGTH, TITLE, VIEWS, ENGAGED VIEWS, VIEWED VS SWIPED AWAY, AVERAGE VIEW DURATION, AVERAGE PERCENT VIEWED, SUBSCRIBERS GAINED]. Identify what is actually supported by the data, avoid claiming correlation proves causation, compare topics and hooks, propose 3 small A/B-style tests for the next week, and generate 8 original video ideas. Do not guarantee reach or income.

## Prompt 6: Permissions request to another video creator
Write a short, respectful message asking [CREATOR] for permission to reuse [SPECIFIC VIDEO URL AND TIMESTAMP] as a brief excerpt in my original commentary Shorts, including possible monetization and distribution to YouTube. Ask for written clarification of scope, attribution requirements, and whether any third-party game audio/music is excluded. Make it easy to say no.
